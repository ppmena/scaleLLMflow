a reliable change in emotional symptoms, and 66.67% a clinically significant change versus 7.14% for both

indicators in the WLC condition. The training effects were longitudinally mediated by the reduction of RNT

focused on clinical practice at two-thirds of the program. An easy-to-implement RNT-focused ACT training is

effective in reducing emotional symptoms and promoting valued living in clinical psychology trainees.
