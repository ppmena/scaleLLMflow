clinicians’ ability to apply course content in clinical simulations. Overall, the results provide strong

support for the efﬁcacy of technology-based OLT methods in disseminating knowledge of empirically

supported treatments to community mental health providers, suggesting that OLT may be a high-quality,

easily accessible, and affordable addition to traditional training methods.
